# Additive Inverse

### Additive Inverse of a Number

The opposite of a number. For example, the additive inverse of 7 is -7. The additive inverse of -2 is 2. Formally, the additive inverse of x is -x. Note: The summ of a number and its additive inverse is 0.

#### Key Formula

x + (-x) = 0

**Where:**
x = Any real number
-x = The additive inverse (opposite) of x
